## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **1. Generate Analytics**
1. Go to the Analytics page
2. Choose analysis type (comprehensive, sentiment, trends, performance)
3. Select time range (all, week, month, quarter)
4. Click "Generate" button
5. View your analytics immediately!

### **2. Export Data**
1. Click "Show History" to see analytics history
2. Click "Export" button
3. Choose format (JSON, CSV, PDF, Excel)
4. Click "Export" to download

### **3. Delete Analytics**
1. Click "Show History" to see analytics history
2. Click trash icon to delete individual analytics
3. Click "Clear All" to delete all analytics

### **4. Real-time Mode**
1. Toggle "Real-time" button
2. Enable "Auto-refresh" for continuous updates
3. Watch live metrics and charts
